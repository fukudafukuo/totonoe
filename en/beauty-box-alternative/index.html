Beauty Box Alternative for Premiere Pro and After Effects (2026)

Totonoe Skin Retouch is a lower-priced alternative to Beauty Box Video for Premiere Pro and After Effects. It is a one-time purchase of ¥9,800 (about US$65), and Digital Anarchy's store lists Beauty Box Video 6.0 at $199 for one host app. Both detect skin by color and smooth only the skin area, but they differ in host support and extra tools.

I am a video editor in Japan (music videos, interviews and commercials) and the developer of Totonoe Skin Retouch. I have not tested Beauty Box on the same footage, so the Beauty Box side of this comparison is based on information Digital Anarchy publishes. Prices were checked on November 8, 2026.

Short answer

  • If you edit in Premiere Pro or After Effects and want to spend less, Totonoe Skin Retouch costs about one third of the Beauty Box Video price listed on Digital Anarchy's store.
  • If you need DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro or Avid, or the extra AI tools for eyes, mouth and teeth, Beauty Box Video is the better choice.
  • Both offer a trial with a watermark, so you can compare them on your own footage before paying.

What is the same and what is different

The same

  • Both find skin by its color and build a mask, so smoothing is limited to skin.
  • Both are effect plugins for Premiere Pro and After Effects, so you apply them from the Effects panel like any other effect.
  • Both offer a trial, so you can test them on your own footage.

Different

  • Hosts. Totonoe supports only Premiere Pro and After Effects. Beauty Box also supports Final Cut Pro, Avid and DaVinci Resolve.
  • Extra tools. Beauty Box lists AI-based masks, AI detection of eyes, mouth and teeth, and teeth and eye whitening. Totonoe is a single skin retouch effect with no face detection.
  • Setup effort. Totonoe has presets (Natural, Standard, Strong) and one Amount control. Skin is detected the moment you apply the effect, so most shots need no masking.
  • Texture. Totonoe uses edge-preserving smoothing (a guided filter) to keep eyes, brows, lashes and hair edges sharp, and a Texture control adds back fine pore texture. De-Shine and Blemish controls are separate, and Temporal Stability steadies detection in moving shots.
  • Bit depth. Totonoe works in 8, 16 and 32-bit.

When to choose which

Beauty Box Video is the better choice if

  • You edit in DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro or Avid.
  • You want AI masks for eyes, mouth and teeth, or whitening tools, in the same plugin.
  • You want a product from an established vendor with a longer history.

Totonoe Skin Retouch is the better choice if

  • You work only in Premiere Pro or After Effects and want to keep the cost low.
  • You want a result in a few clicks, with presets and one Amount control.
  • You want footage to stay on your computer, with no network use by the plugin.

Limits of Totonoe Skin Retouch

  • It detects skin by color, so everyone with a similar skin tone in the shot is retouched. To treat one person, add a mask in Premiere Pro or After Effects.
  • Log footage needs a Rec.709 conversion before the effect.
  • It does not run in DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro or Avid.

FAQ

What is a cheaper alternative to Beauty Box Video?

Totonoe Skin Retouch is a one-time purchase of ¥9,800 (about US$65) for Premiere Pro and After Effects. Digital Anarchy's store lists Beauty Box Video 6.0 at $199 for one host app as of November 8, 2026.

Does Totonoe Skin Retouch work in DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro or Avid?

No. Totonoe Skin Retouch supports only Premiere Pro and After Effects 2025 or later. Digital Anarchy lists Beauty Box Video for DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro and Avid as well.

Can I try Totonoe Skin Retouch before buying?

Yes. The free trial on BOOTH has every feature of the full version and adds a faint diagonal-stripe watermark to the video. Digital Anarchy also offers a fully functional watermarked trial of Beauty Box Video.

Does Totonoe Skin Retouch send my footage to a server?

No. All processing runs on your computer, footage is never uploaded, and the plugin works offline after installation.

Is Totonoe Skin Retouch the same quality as Beauty Box Video?

I have not tested Beauty Box Video on the same footage, so I cannot say. Both detect skin by color and smooth only the detected area, so the best check is to run each trial on your own footage.
